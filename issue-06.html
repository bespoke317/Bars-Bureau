16K Miles & Runnin'

October 2026

+
§ 01

The Motion

October was scary. Scary good.

The road trip that started in September carried us through mid-October: almost 16,000 miles across London, Berlin, Vienna, and Rome. Every city has its own personality, its own rhythm. But Vienna hit different. It was calming and invigorating at the same time, the kind of place that settles you and wakes you up in the same breath. We'll be back sooner rather than later.

The brand didn't take a day off either. While abroad, we launched Rough Kuts on TikTok: bars to camera, 8 to 16 at a time, with the words synced on screen. No polish, no frills, just the pen. Think of it as the precursor to Bespoke Breakdowns, recorded wherever in the world we happen to be standing.

Firm life kept pace. London, Berlin, and even Rome created space to connect with colleagues, clients, and the broader network, including the World Law Group and the Global Alliance of Impact Lawyers. It's inspiring to see lawyers across the globe still innovating and taking on the problems that deserve attention.

Rome brought a few days of firsts alongside my fiancée. Now we're back in Chicago, where autumn is in full swing, and a few weeks to reset feels like a gift. That stability gives us room to lock in the wedding details. The headline: we're getting married in a castle on the water in Italy. Storybook stuff.

Every issue checks the pulse of where Bespoke317 is right now.

+
§ 02

The Docket

November is a cornucopia.

First stop: Indianapolis. Back home to catch up with colleagues and, equally important, to get back in the booth. Geechie has sent over several tracks that I'm super excited to record, and I can't wait to hear the final product. Lately the work has been coming fast and clean, the product of a true flow state. Efficiency meeting proficiency. So the question has to be asked: LP, anyone?

Another kind of creative inspiration has come from life as a soon-to-be groom.

Here's the general truth. In wedding season, grooms tend to live in relative obscurity. Some are aloof on purpose (that might be a flag). But many have the time, the energy, and the resources. What they lack is guidance on where to put their time, talent, and treasure.

That's where Grūm SZN comes in. It's a service built for men entering their groom season. Free Game and Playbooks are open to everyone, and an optional paid tier goes the extra mile for grooms who want to move through the season with more intention, confidence, and presence. The site is live now in its trial form.

If it's on "The Docket," it's already in motion.

+
§ 03

Off the Record

After 16,000 miles, a lot of firsts, and the quiet of a Chicago autumn, one truth keeps rising to the top:

Gratitude is the highest vibration.

We always have a choice to make, and our experience is shaped by that choice. Every day, in every room, in every city, the same question is waiting.

Will we descend into hellish hatred?
Or rise to glory with gratitude?

The choice is ours.

But don't quote me. It's off the record.
